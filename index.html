Pragya is a UX and Product Designer with a background in illustration and licensed consumer products. She designs experiences that are simple to use, emotionally clear, and thoughtfully structured.

Her work is grounded in strong information architecture, intuitive user flows, and interface design that helps people feel oriented and in control.

Pragya's main skillsets include UX and UI design, research synthesis, prototyping, and visual design. She is known for translating complex information into clear, digestible experiences, and for designing with real world constraints in mind.
